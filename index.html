Case Study: 001

Engineering a calm engine for the solo operator.

Klynt is a high-fidelity business management interface designed to eliminate the friction of client tracking and invoicing for creative founders.

ROLE

Product Design, Brand Identity

CATEGORY

SaaS / FinTech

PLATFORM

Web / Desktop App

TOOLS

Figma, React, Tailwind

The Challenge

The Administrative Tax.

Most business tools for freelancers are either too "friendly" and lack depth, or too enterprise-heavy and cause cognitive overload. Founders spend 20% of their week just trying to remember who has paid and who hasn't.

Objective

Reduce Friction

Every interaction in Klynt was measured by "time-to-clarity." We aimed to make client status visible in under 2 seconds.

Visual Identity

High-Contrast Trust

Using deep blacks and mint accents creates a premium environment that signals stability and control.

The Core Experience

One Screen. Zero Noise.

The dashboard focuses on the "Founder's Vitals"—Cash flow, pending tasks, and immediate actions.
